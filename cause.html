Our partner

Heart for Monroe

Heart for Monroe works at the point where need is most immediate: a hot meal, a box of food, a coat that fits. They do it by connecting the organizations already in Monroe rather than duplicating them, which is why a clothing donation reaches someone quickly instead of sitting in storage.

That is why we chose them. We wanted the distance between a bag leaving someone’s house and a person wearing what’s inside it to be as short as possible.
