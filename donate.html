      Before you bag it

      Five steps, printed like a care label

      Every hour we spend re-washing or unpicking a knotted bag is an hour we’re not sorting. Following these five steps genuinely doubles what we can process in an afternoon.

      The labeling step matters most. A bag marked “Kids 4–6” goes straight onto the right rack. An unmarked bag has to be opened, emptied and sorted item by item.
