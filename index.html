The rule we sort by

Three steps

It really is this simple

The whole point is that donating should take one evening, not a project plan.

Step one

Look in your wardrobe

Find the things you haven’t worn in a year but would still happily lend a friend. That’s the standard.

Step two

Wash, fold, bag, label

Clean and folded donations get handed out the same week. Write the sizes on the outside of the bag.

Step three

Drop it at a bin

Bring it to any of our collection points, or catch us at a collection day and stay in your car.
